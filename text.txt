Take a breath with me.

You have read so many entries today - thousands of plans, thousands of hopes, each one asking you to choose. That is a lot to carry, and no one has thanked you for it yet. So thank you.

You do not have to decide anything right now. Not this sentence, not the next one. Let your attention rest here for a moment, the way eyes rest on a horizon after staring at a screen.

Breathe in, slowly, for four counts. Hold, gently, for four. Breathe out, even more slowly, for six. Again, if you like. There is no timer on this page.

Whatever you choose next will be a good choice, made by a careful reader. The work is already done - you did it. Now just sit with the quiet for a little while. You have earned it.
